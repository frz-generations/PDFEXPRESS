No server-side quotas

No account or daily server cap.

Most free PDF sites cap you at a handful of files a day, then ask for an account or a subscription to keep going. That cap exists because their tools run on a server — every file you send them costs real bandwidth and processing time, so they ration it. FRZ PDF Express never uploads a file anywhere, so there's nothing to ration: your own device does the work, as many times as you need it to. Frequently asked questions

Is FRZ PDF Express free to use?

Yes. FRZ PDF Express is free, needs no sign-up and has no imposed daily file-processing cap. Very large files are still limited by the memory available on your own device.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. PDF and Office files are processed in your browser on your device. The site does not upload your documents.

Can I scan pages with my phone camera?

Yes. In Scan to PDF, tap Start rear camera to request camera permission, capture multiple pages, adjust crop handles, batch-edit selected pages, choose A4, A5, Letter or Legal, and create one PDF. Camera permission is not requested unless you tap the camera button, and images stay on your device.

Does FRZ PDF Express work offline?

After the site and its client-side libraries load once, the app shell and cached tools can work offline. OCR language data may need its first online download.

Can I delete pages from a PDF?

Open Delete PDF Pages or Organize Pages, select the page thumbnails, choose Delete selected pages, then download the new PDF. The original file remains unchanged.

Which spreadsheet formats can I convert to PDF?

Spreadsheet to PDF accepts XLSX, XLS, CSV and TSV. Each sheet becomes paginated PDF tables; sheets wider than 12 columns continue across pages, and very wide cell values may be shortened to fit.

Does PowerPoint to PDF preserve slide design?

The browser-only converter extracts readable text from modern PPTX files into PDF pages. Complex layouts, images, animations and charts are not reproduced, and legacy PPT files are not supported.

Can PDF pages be converted to PowerPoint?

It creates one image-based slide per PDF page, preserving page proportions with white margins when needed. The slides are not editable text or shapes.

Can PDF text be converted to a spreadsheet?

Yes. Selectable text is grouped into rows and approximate columns in an XLSX workbook, one sheet per page. Scanned PDFs need OCR first, and table reconstruction is best effort.

Does Crop PDF permanently remove page content?

No. Crop PDF changes the visible crop box. Content outside that area remains in the PDF and may be restored by another editor.

Can I add a signature to a PDF?

Yes. Draw or type a visual signature and place it on a page. It is not a certificate-backed digital signature and does not send a signature request.

Can FRZ PDF Express compare two PDFs?

It compares selectable text page by page and reports page counts and pages present in only one PDF, including blank or image-only extra pages. It does not compare images or visual layout; scanned pages need OCR first.

Can Word documents be converted to PDF?

Modern .docx files are converted locally from their paragraphs and basic headings. Complex formatting, columns, tables and images may not be preserved; legacy .doc is not supported. Built-in PDF fonts support English and basic Latin text best, so some other scripts may be replaced.

Can a PDF be converted to Word?

Selectable text can be exported to a basic .docx with paragraphs and page breaks. The original layout and images are not recreated, and scanned PDFs need OCR first.

Can a PDF be converted to Markdown?

Yes. Selectable PDF text is exported with page headings and paragraph breaks. Tables and semantic structure are best effort; scanned pages need OCR first.
