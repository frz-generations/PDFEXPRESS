Accessibility statement

FRZ Generations aims to make FRZ PDF Express usable by everyone, including people who use screen readers, keyboards or magnification. The application is built to the WCAG 2.1 Level AA success criteria, which is the accessibility basis of the Guidelines for Indian Government Websites and Apps (GIGW 3.0) and of the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016.

  • All tools can be operated with a keyboard; file drop zones can be opened with Enter or Space.
  • Visible keyboard focus, a “Skip to main content” link, labelled form fields, and status messages announced to screen readers.
  • Light and dark themes, a reduce-motion setting, and respect for the system “reduce motion” preference.
  • Pages are responsive and work on phones and desktops; text can be zoomed to 200% without loss of function.

Status of conformance: checked by the creators with automated testing (axe-core) and manual keyboard testing. It has not yet been audited by an independent or government-empanelled accessibility auditor. Known limitations: the interface is in English only; the PDF page-preview tools (viewer, organise, crop, sign, mark-up) are visual by nature and have limited screen-reader support — use “Extract text” and the converters for text access; some tools need a pointer to position items on a page.

If you find an accessibility barrier, please write to frzgenerations@gmail.com and describe the page, the tool and the assistive technology you use.

Privacy policy

  • No uploads. PDFs, images and Office files you choose are processed on your own device, in your browser. They are not sent to FRZ Generations or to any server.
  • No accounts, no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party requests. All libraries, fonts and OCR language data are served from this application’s own files.
  • What is stored on your device: your theme choice and a few settings (browser local storage), and an offline copy of the application (service-worker cache). You can remove them any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser.
  • Camera: the phone-scan tool asks for camera access only after you tap “Start rear camera”, and stops the camera when you stop, leave the tool or hide the app. Photos stay on your device.
  • Because the application does not collect or transmit personal data, FRZ Generations holds no personal data of its users. If you host your own copy, you are responsible for how your host handles web-server logs.

Security

The application is static (no server code, no database) and ships a Content Security Policy that blocks requests to other websites and inline-object content. To report a vulnerability, email frzgenerations@gmail.com; see security.txt. Please allow reasonable time for a fix before public disclosure.

Help and contact
